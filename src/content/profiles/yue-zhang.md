Yue Zhang is Ph.D. candidate at Guangdong University of Technology, as a visiting student studying here. She received her Bachelor in Mechanical engineering from Guangdong University of Technology. Her research interests focus on biomedical design and manufacturing, soft and hard tissue cutting mechanics, and orthopedic surgery.
